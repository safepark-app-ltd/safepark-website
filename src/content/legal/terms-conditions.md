If an account does not meet these conditions, we may cancel the subscription and close the account. If this happens during the free trial, you will not be charged.

You can close your account at any time from the My Account area of The App.

## Free trial and subscription

This section applies to drivers who subscribe for themselves. Haulier accounts, and drivers who join through a haulier, are covered in the next two sections.

New accounts include a 7-day free trial. We take your card details when you sign up, but you will not be charged during the trial. We will email you before your trial ends to remind you that your subscription is about to start. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription starts and you will be charged the price shown when you signed up, then again at the start of each billing period until you cancel.

Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card details.

Prices may change, and we may run promotions from time to time. We will tell you about any price change at least 7 days before the next payment it applies to.

You can cancel at any time from the My Account area. Cancelling stops future payments, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. We do not refund part of a billing period.

## Haulier accounts

A haulier can buy licences for its drivers. Each licence lets one driver use The App. The haulier's own login is free and does not use a licence.

**Free trial.** New haulier accounts include a 7-day free trial. We take your card details when you sign up, but you will not be charged during the trial. We will email you before the trial ends. If you do not cancel before then, you will be charged for the number of licences you have when the trial ends, and then again at the start of each monthly billing period until you cancel. If your account was moved to licences from our earlier service, your trial starts without a card. You need to add one before the trial ends, or access stops for you and your drivers.

**Prices.** The price is per licence per month. It is shown before VAT, and VAT is added at the UK rate.

**Changing the number of licences.** You can change the number of licences at any time from your dashboard.

- During the free trial, changes are free. You pay for the number you have when the trial ends.
- After the trial, extra licences are charged straight away for the rest of the current billing period, then at the full price from the next one.
- Fewer licences take effect straight away, and the lower price applies from your next payment. We do not refund part of a billing period.
- You cannot have fewer licences than the drivers using them. Remove drivers first.

**Your haulier code.** Your dashboard gives you a link and a code to send your drivers. You are responsible for who you share them with. You can make a new code at any time; the old one then stops working, and drivers who have already joined keep their access. Each driver who joins uses one licence. You can suspend or remove a driver at any time. A suspended driver still uses a licence; a removed driver does not.

**Payments.** If a payment fails, you and your drivers keep access for 7 days while you update your card. After that, access stops for you and all your drivers until the payment is made.

**Cancelling.** You can cancel at any time from your dashboard. Cancelling stops future payments, and you and your drivers keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After that, your drivers stay linked to your account but cannot use The App unless you buy licences again.

**Your responsibility for your drivers.** You must only invite people who drive for you, and you must have told them that you will see the details set out in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Drivers who join through a haulier

If your haulier gives you a link or code, you can join their account. This is free for you: your haulier pays for your licence.

- When you join, we share your name and email address with your haulier, and they can see that you use SafePark. We tell you this before you join.
- Your access depends on your haulier. They can suspend or remove you, and if they cancel or their payment fails, your access stops.
- You can disconnect from your haulier at any time from The App. If you then use their code again, they need to approve you before you get access.
- While you are linked to a haulier you cannot subscribe for yourself. Disconnect first.

The App shows secure parking locations and reported crime across the UK and Europe. Parking information, including facilities, prices and accreditations, is provided as a guide. Arrangements and payment for parking are made directly with the site operator, and The Company is not party to them.

Crime data is drawn from police and other sources and is provided as a guide to risk. The Company does not guarantee that it is complete or current, and it does not replace your own judgement about where it is safe to stop.

Accepted reports are shown to other users anonymously, without your name or any details that identify you. The Company may edit or decline any report.
