## Parking providers

Those parking providers registered with The Company will have access to the app for the purpose of booking parking for drivers represented by SafePark customers. Parking providers will only book parking for vehicle registrations that are present at the parking provider. Parking providers must not use alternative vehicle details in order to bypass the correct billing process to SafePark customers – the Hauliers.

Parking Providers are responsible for the maintenance of the services available, and their prices, using The App. All prices must include any relevant VAT. All parking providers must be VAT registered unless a special exemption has been granted by The Company. When booking requests are made the prices at the time of booking are to be used, and may not be changed once a booking has been made.

Parking Providers have the right to refuse entry to any vehicle whether parking has been booked or not. The Parking Provider agrees to inform The Company of this within 7 days.

Purchase Orders shall be raised at agreed intervals, containing a breakdown of parking and services that have been booked through The App. The Company must receive a corresponding invoice in order to settle any accounts, which will be on 30 day terms unless otherwise agreed in writing at the commencement of the agreement.

There is no minimum or contracted term to be part of the SafePark network. Membership can be cancelled at any time by contacting The Company. It can take up to 7 days to remove a parking location from SafePark, and any parking booked in this time shall still be subject to settlement by Purchase Order and invoice.

## Hauliers

Hauliers are responsible for managing the vehicles who may use the SafePark system. It is the vehicle, not the driver, which is used for billing. It is prohibited from adding vehicle details for any vehicles where the Haulier is not authorised to pay for parking.

Vehicles are identified by registration, and can only be associated with a single Haulier. Where a vehicle is shared between multiple Hauliers one must be responsible for that vehicle on SafePark’s systems.

Parking requests, whether by the driver or the haulier, will result in an invoice within 7 days, which must be paid within 14 days, unless otherwise agreed by The Company.

There is no minimum or contracted term to be part of the SafePark network. Membership can be cancelled at any time by contacting The Company. It can take up to 7 days to remove a haulier from SafePark, and any parking booked in this time shall still be subject to settlement by Invoice.

The Company will maintain the integrity of the data in the application, as well as the functionality of the application. Parking Service Providers and Hauliers have the right to be removed from the system, and need only contact The Company to do this.
