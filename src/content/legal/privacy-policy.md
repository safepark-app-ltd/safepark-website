## About this policy

This privacy policy explains what personal data SafePark collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your rights over it. It covers this website (safepark.app) and the SafePark map (map.safepark.app).

## Who we are

SafePark is run by SafePark App Ltd, 164 Northwood Lane, Newcastle, ST5 4BZ. We are the controller of your personal data, and we are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) under number ZB920897.

“We”, “us” and “our” mean SafePark App Ltd. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email us at support@safepark.app.

## What we collect

### Details you give us

- **When you sign up or request access:** your name, email address and phone number, where you drive, and, if you choose to tell us, how you heard about us, your company and anything else you want us to know.
- **When you contact us:** your name, email address and your message.
- **When you subscribe:** your card details, which go straight to our payment provider, Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. We keep a record of your subscription and payments.
- **When you report an incident:** the type of incident, its location and time, and any description you add.

### Details collected when you use the map

- **Your location:** with your permission, the map uses your device’s location to centre the map and show where you are. Your browser asks for permission first, and you can turn live location off from the icon on the map. Your location is not stored by us or linked to your account. To draw the map around you, Mapbox receives requests for that area of the map.
- **Technical data:** like most online services, our servers record your IP address, browser and device type, and the date and time of your requests. We use this to keep the service secure and working.
- **Your settings:** favourites, filters and display settings are stored on your own device. See our [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/).

We do not use analytics or advertising tracking.

We do not sell your data. We share it with others only where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from the police or a court, or to protect the rights, property or safety of SafePark, our users or the public.

If SafePark App Ltd is sold or merged with another business, your data may be transferred to the new owner. We will tell you before that happens.

## International transfers

Some of our processors hold data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge) or by contracts approved for the UK, such as the International Data Transfer Addendum.

## How long we keep your data

- **Your account:** for as long as you have one. We delete your account data within 30 days of you closing your account.
- **Payment records:** 6 years, as required by tax law.
- **Access requests that do not become accounts:** 12 months.
- **Incident reports:** kept, because they form part of the crime picture on the map. When you close your account, the link between you and your reports is removed.
- **Technical data:** for a short time only, unless we need it to investigate a security problem.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- ask us to correct anything that is wrong
- ask us to delete your data
- object to us using your data, or ask us to restrict how we use it
- ask us to send your data to you, or to another organisation, in a common format
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

You can update your details and close your account from the My Account area of the map. For anything else, email support@safepark.app. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please contact us before you do.

## Security

We protect your data with encryption, access controls and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure, but we take every reasonable step to keep your data safe.

## Age limit

SafePark is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

## Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, such as driver welfare organisations. We are not responsible for their content or how they handle your data, so please read their privacy policies.

## Changes to this policy

If we make important changes to this policy, we will email you or show a notice in the map before they take effect, and update the date at the top of this page.
