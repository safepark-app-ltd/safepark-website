Every year, cargo theft costs the logistics industry billions. Truck drivers and hauliers are particularly vulnerable when forced to park in unsecured locations, especially during peak holiday seasons when freight volumes are at their highest.

## The scale of the problem

- **Cargo theft losses:** over £50 million a year in the UK alone, with truck stops and lay-bys as primary targets.
- **Frequency of incidents:** around 30% of all cargo theft incidents happen while vehicles are parked in unsecured locations.
- **Seasonal spikes:** incidents increase by up to 40% during November and December, as thieves target high-value goods like electronics and holiday retail stock.

## Why safe, reliable parking matters

1. **Financial losses:** a single incident can cost a haulier up to £100,000, including the value of goods, vehicle damage and insurance premiums.
2. **Driver safety:** over 20% of cargo theft incidents involve threats or harm to the driver.
3. **Operational disruption:** delays caused by theft result in missed deadlines and strained client relationships.
4. **Insurance impact:** claims from thefts in unsecured areas can lead to higher premiums or loss of cover.
